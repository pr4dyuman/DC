# Search data review: 10 August to 8 November 2026

Written 10 November 2026. This is the first measurement for step 8 of
`doc/visitor-growth-plan.md`.

The data comes from Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4, read through
Windsor.ai. Search Console had data up to 8 November. The Jaipur titles and
headings from the plan went live on 10 November, so these numbers are the
"before" picture.

## In short

- Google showed the site 7,475 times and 40 people clicked. That is a click
  rate of 0.54% at an average position of 16.4.
- Almost none of those impressions are possible clients. 95% came from outside
  India, and one article about SEO data APIs accounts for about 60% of them.
  Most of those searches look automated, not typed by people.
- People in India saw the site 363 times and clicked 32 times (8.8%). Most
  clicks open the homepage, which points to people searching for the brand.
- The Jaipur searches in the keyword plan produced no impressions. Google
  reported two "SEO agency Jaipur" searches in three months, one impression
  each.
- So the usual quick wins are thin. The ones worth doing are a Jaipur SEO
  article that already sits at position 13, five articles with a broken
  search snippet, and one article worth rewriting. See "Suggested page
  changes".

### Why most of these searches look automated

- 151 of the 343 named searches are worded like prompts to an AI assistant or
  a tracking tool: "i am an seo professional, recommend me best seo data api",
  "best seo data api. my location is usa.", "3200: tracking seo api".
- Named searches arrive at the same rate on weekends as on weekdays (58 and
  63 impressions a day).
- They produced 2 clicks from 5,638 impressions. One phrase was shown 73 times
  at position 2 without a click.
- 88% of all impressions are on desktop, spread across 108 countries.

This cannot be proved from Search Console alone, but people searching for a
supplier do not behave like this.

## Suggested page changes

In order of value.

### 1. The Jaipur SEO article

`/blog/best-seo-agency-in-jaipur-choose-an-ethical-partner` is the only page
that Indian searchers looking for an SEO agency already see. Google has shown
it since 23 October: 43 impressions in 17 days at position 12.7, no clicks yet.

Change in the blog editor:

- **Meta title:** from "Best SEO Agency in Jaipur - Choose an Ethical
  Partner" to "Best SEO Agency in Jaipur: How to Choose and What It Costs".
- **Meta description:** "How to choose an SEO agency in Jaipur: what ethical,
  white-hat SEO includes, what it should cost, red flags to avoid and
  questions to ask before you sign."
- **Add a short section on what SEO costs in Jaipur**, with ₹ ranges. One of
  the two Jaipur searches was "affordable seo agency in jaipur". This needs
  the starting prices from step 5 of the plan.
- **Fix the heading** "The Jaipur SEO Agency Evaluation Scorecard: A a
  structured sequence Vetting Framework". Suggested: "Jaipur SEO agency
  scorecard: what to check before you sign".
- **Drop "400%"** from the heading "…Sustainable 400% Organic Growth" unless
  it is a real client result. An article about ethical SEO should not promise
  a number.
- **Change one early link** to `/services/seo` from the words "seo services"
  to "SEO company in Jaipur", the service page's main search.

### 2. Link the SEO service page to that article

`/services/seo` lists three guides. None is the Jaipur article, and one is the
technical SEO article at position 76. Replace that entry with the Jaipur
article in `relatedArticles` in `lib/marketing-service-pages/seo.ts`.

### 3. Five articles with a broken search snippet

Five recent articles use their full headline as the meta title, so Google cuts
it off. Three already sit between positions 5 and 20.

| Article | Position | Problem | Suggested meta title |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `technical-seo-vs-on-page-key-implementation-priorities` | 75.8 | Title is 106 characters. Description is only "Defining Technical SEO and On-Page SEO Core Boundaries." | Technical SEO vs On-Page SEO: Differences and Priorities |
| `track-instagram-creator-affiliate-sales` | 9.0 | Title stops mid-phrase: "…: A Setup And Budget". | How to Track Instagram Creator Affiliate Sales: Codes & UTMs |
| `build-local-citations-structured-outreach-spreadsheet-template` | 6.8 | Title is 67 characters. | How to Build Local Citations: Outreach Spreadsheet Template |
| `influencer-collaboration-contracts-usage-rights` | 10.6 | Title is 68 characters. | Influencer Collaboration Contracts: Usage Rights Explained |
| `customized-web-application-development-architecture` | no impressions yet | Title is 83 characters. | Custom Web Application Architecture for Growing Businesses |

For the first one, a suggested description: "What technical SEO and on-page
SEO each cover, how they differ, and which to fix first on your website, with
a simple order of priorities."

New articles should arrive with a meta title under 60 characters, so this does
not repeat.

### 4. Rewrite the 2026 social media trends article

It was shown 553 times at position 8 for one click. The June report shows the
same pattern for the spring: 251 impressions at position 7.8 and no clicks. In
October it slipped to position 14, and in November it was shown once.

The plan already asks for TikTok to be removed. Do the rewrite in December,
when people start searching for next year's trends:

- **Meta title:** "Social Media Marketing Trends 2027 for Indian Businesses".
- **Meta description:** "The social media trends that matter for Indian
  businesses in 2027: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp, creators and
  AI content, with what to do first."
- **Content:** lead with Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp, use Indian examples,
  and remove TikTok.
- **Keep the same address**, so the page keeps its position.

### 5. Retire the US plumbers article

`/blog/local-service-ads-vs-google-business-plumber-leads` was shown 38 times
at position 55 with no clicks. The searches Google names are all about ads for
plumbers. It is also one of the three "Latest Articles" on the homepage and one
of three guides on `/services/ppc`.

- Add it to `redirects` in `lib/marketing-blog-removed-posts.json`, pointing to
  `/services/ppc`. That also takes it off the homepage and the blog list.
- Replace it in `relatedArticles` in `lib/marketing-service-pages/ppc.ts`.

### 6. Leave the SEO data APIs article alone

It brings impressions, not clients, and no Jaipur business owner searches for
SEO data APIs. Do not write more articles on this topic. Its readers are SEO
developers, so if it should earn anything, point its call to action at
zSEO.ai rather than agency services.

### 7. Optional: Agency OS

The old Agency OS page was shown for "agency management platform", "ad agency
management software" and "best agency management system" at positions 2 to 9,
a few impressions each. It now redirects to `/agency-os`. The new page has the
phrase in its title but not in its heading, which reads "manage your company
with Agency OS". Put "agency management software" in the heading or the first
paragraph.

### No change yet: the service pages

The six service pages had 213 impressions and no clicks, and Google names no
Jaipur search for any of them. Their new Jaipur titles went live on 10
November, so there is nothing to tune until Google has shown them for a few
weeks.

## What this means for the keyword plan

- **The keyword map stands.** Nothing in the data argues against it. The
  starting point for every Jaipur search in it is zero.
- **The first blog topic should be SEO cost** ("how much does SEO cost in
  India"). The only Jaipur searches so far included "affordable".
- **Keep the words "ethical" and "white-hat"** on the SEO page and article.
  Indian searchers used them, and the site already ranks 10 to 14 for them.
- **Stop publishing for foreign developers.** New articles should come only
  from the plan's list of topics for Indian business owners.
- **The biggest gaps are still off the website:** Google Business Profile,
  directory listings and reviews, as the plan says. Analytics counted 24
  visitors from India in three months, so no title change will matter as
  much.

## Notes on the data

- **Property used:** the domain property `digitalcorvids.com`. Two other
  connected properties are parts of it: `https://digitalcorvids.com/` (36
  clicks, 7,381 impressions) and `https://www.digitalcorvids.com/` (nothing).
- **Hidden searches:** search-level rows cover 5,638 impressions and 2 clicks.
  The other 38 clicks came from searches Google does not name.
- **Page by country and page by device:** Windsor adds the search words to
  these requests, so they lose the same hidden clicks. This review uses only
  the country and device totals, which match the property total.
- **Page totals:** counted page by page there are 41 clicks and 7,939
  impressions, because one search can show two pages.
- **How it was pulled:** `https://connectors.windsor.ai/searchconsole` and
  `/googleanalytics4`, with `date_from=2026-08-10`, `date_to=2026-11-10` and
  the fields listed by each connector's `/fields` address. The key is read
  from `WINDSOR_API_KEY`.
